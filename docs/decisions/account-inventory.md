## Agreed inventory boundaries

Each requested category has an owner/operator category, required data and a proposed manual statement-entry, valuation or candidate CSV route recorded privately. Registered legal title and exact source export support are activation checks, not facts invented by the app. All connectivity is `unverified`; default sharing is NONE. Family use does not establish joint legal ownership or partner disclosure consent.

Spaces must not be added to a parent balance until inclusion is verified. Child-earmarked money must not be counted as adult spendable funds. The app must distinguish verified ownership, reported beneficiary intent and managing authority.
