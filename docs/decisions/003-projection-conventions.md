# Projection contract and calculation conventions (Task 3A)

Status: accepted for the contract foundation, 23 October 2026. The calculator and API are later tasks. All contract objects carry a literal `schema` name and `schemaVersion: 1`; parsers reject unknown fields.

## Money, rates, and calendar

- Source-row Money is `{ amountMinor: string, currency: "GBP" }` within signed 64-bit range. Canonical minor-unit strings have no leading zero, decimal point, exponent, or negative zero. Opening balance, goal target, planned contributions, withdrawals, fixed fees, tax, and fixed allocations use this range. Required nonnegative fields reject a minus sign.
- Aggregated wealth and result totals use the existing 40-digit `AggregateMoney` contract, because summing valid source rows can exceed signed 64-bit range. Result growth can be negative; balances, targets and other breakdown amounts are nonnegative. No FX or non-GBP currency is accepted.
- Decimal rates and allocation fractions are canonical strings with at most 12 fractional places: no exponent, leading zero, redundant trailing fractional zero, or negative zero. Fractions are in `(0, 1]`, where `0.25` means 25% of a named eligible valuation. Exact scaled-integer comparisons enforce bounds. Gross effective annual growth is `[-1, 1]`; proportional fee is `[0, 1)`; inflation is `(-1, 1]`. The 100% annual upper bound keeps a signed-64-bit opening amount compounded for 600 months within the 40-digit aggregate result contract. Each rate has its own provenance: `sourceKind` (`USER`, `VERIFIED_PROVIDER`, `ILLUSTRATION`), nullable `sourceRef`, effective date and `EFFECTIVE_ANNUAL` convention. Only a verified provider rate requires a nonempty source reference. This contract does not claim that any provider is approved or connected.
- Date-only fields use ISO `YYYY-MM-DD` and reject impossible dates and year zero. First contributions fall at the end of a Europe/London calendar month. A schedule is bounded to 1..600 months and must stay within year 9999. A contribution plan reserves the full 600-month calendar span.

## Version 1 wire shapes

`WealthSummaryDto` identifies household, `scope: { kind: "VISIBLE", accountIds, datasetVersion, grantVersion }`, completeness, excluded reasons, manual valuation references (`accountId`, `valuationId`, `asOfDate`, `source: "MANUAL"`), as-of date, adult net worth, and liquid adult assets. Visible account IDs are unique; each valuation belongs to the visible scope, and each account and valuation appears at most once. It contains no inaccessible-account count or hidden account identifiers. `PARTIAL` means a visible scoped source is missing or stale; scope is selected by an authorised caller before building the DTO. This contract does not claim bank or broker feeds, FX, child ownership, or an executable wealth calculator.

`GoalDto` identifies household and goal, name, `ACTIVE | ARCHIVED` lifecycle, adult beneficiary, priority, optimistic version, nonnegative GBP target and optional target date. `GoalAllocationSetDto` identifies the same household/goal and version and carries bounded allocations, at most one per source account within the set. `FIXED` has GBP `amount`; `FRACTION` has canonical `fraction`; both name an eligible `accountId` and `valuationId`. These are funding intentions, not transfers or spendable cash. Database and service work must verify matching household, account, valuation and grant before accepting them.

`ContributionPlanDto` identifies household/goal, version, first month-end and ordered, nonempty monthly contribution steps `{ fromMonth, amount }`. The first step begins at month 1; each later step replaces the amount from its month onward. Amounts are fixed nominal GBP and do not automatically rise with inflation.

`ProjectionInputV1` has opening and target GBP, target basis, first month-end, horizon `1..600`, and ordered monthly contributions. `BASIC` has only these fields and requires `FIXED_NOMINAL`; unknown advanced assumptions are rejected. `ADVANCED` also requires a saved `scenarioId`, ordered scheduled withdrawals, fixed fees and tax, effective annual gross growth, proportional fee and inflation rates, and independent provenance for all three rates. These explicit schedules are assumptions, never observed ledger entries. `TODAYS_MONEY` is available only in ADVANCED. Scenario DTOs carry an immutable advanced input, identity, version and creation time. `ProjectionRunDto` carries household/goal, mode, nullable `scenarioId` only for BASIC, full input/result, SHA-256 input hash, engine version, `HALF_UP_MINOR_UNIT` convention, source dataset/grant revisions, manual valuation references and creation time. Storage must preserve prior runs and scenarios when goals or source valuations change; DTO validation alone does not enforce immutability.

`ProjectionResultV1` has exactly one calculation state: `already_achieved`, `reachable`, `insufficient_inputs`, `budget_deficit`, `depleted`, `unreachable_under_assumptions`, or `beyond_horizon`. It contains nullable first qualifying date, bounded month-end series of closing balance and target, opening/contribution/withdrawal/growth/proportional-fee/fixed-fee/tax/closing breakdown, and enumerated warnings. Series months are unique and consecutive from month 1. A stored run requires each series date to match its input month-end schedule and remain within the horizon. For a reachable run, the first qualifying date identifies the first series entry whose closing balance meets that entry's target; an already-achieved run may use a null date. The later calculator defines arithmetic and other state-specific invariants. Authentication and grant failures are API errors, not result states.

## Engine convention to implement later

The BASIC baseline uses zero growth and no inflation, fee or tax modelling. ADVANCED uses effective annual rates, not nominal APR. At each Europe/London month end, apply monthly gross growth and proportional fee drag to the previous closing balance, then the scheduled contribution, withdrawal, fixed fee and tax. The intended factor is `((1 + grossAnnualRate) * (1 - proportionalFeeAnnualRate))^(1/12)`. Round to integer minor units with HALF_UP at the documented monthly cash boundary; do not round original source money. A depleted balance cannot become a loan. For `TODAYS_MONEY`, inflate the original target by `(1 + inflationAnnualRate)^(m/12)` and compare nominal values; do not deflate the balance and inflate the target simultaneously. Fixed contributions stay nominal.

Golden dates: £1,000 opening plus £250 on each month end reaches £4,000 at the twelfth contribution: 31 October 2027 when the first is 30 November 2026. £290 reaches it at the eleventh: 30 September 2027. Month ends must respect February and leap years. A target already met has no contribution wait. No engine result is produced by this contract-only step.
