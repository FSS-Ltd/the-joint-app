## Product and intended use

The Joint App is a UK-first, read-only household finance organiser for two adults using separate identities. Each adult separately consents to their source accounts and controls partner visibility. Initial scope is current/savings products and provider-managed spaces where supported. Child-profile functionality is post-MVP. Existing child-designated/earmarked funds must still be kept out of adult spendable totals; exact authority and source semantics require verification.

The app does not initiate payments, transfers or trades. Account data would be stored in a UK-region cloud application, with the intended primary application/database location in London. Deterministic calculations are independent of an optional later local-AI companion: only minimal, owner-authorised evidence would be processed on an approved household computer. No cloud-AI fallback is planned. Please confirm whether that processing/partner-display model is permitted and what contractual conditions apply.

Initial pilot is one household/two consenting adults. Please quote separately for hypothetical 100 and 1,000 households and identify every charging unit. Public commercial release is a later gated milestone; there are no existing paying-customer or usage claims.
